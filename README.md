# FarmX

**Empowering farmers. Eliminating middlemen. Enabling fair trade.**

---

## Overview

FarmX is a modern platform that connects farmers directly with buyers such as shops, restaurants, and wholesalers.
By removing intermediaries, it ensures **better profits for farmers** and **fair pricing for buyers**.

---

## Problem Statement

Farmers often struggle to get fair prices for their produce due to middlemen dominating the supply chain.
This results in:

* Lower income for farmers
* Higher prices for buyers
* Lack of transparency

---

## Solution

FarmX provides a **direct digital marketplace** where:

* Farmers can list crops with their own pricing
* Buyers can explore and purchase directly
* Data-driven insights guide better decisions

---

## Key Features

* 🌱 **Crop Listings**

  * Add crops with price, quantity, and details

* 📍 **Location-Based Matching**

  * Find nearby buyers/sellers for efficient trade

* 📊 **Search & Filtering**

   * Users can filter crops dynamically by:

     1)location
     2) Crop Name
